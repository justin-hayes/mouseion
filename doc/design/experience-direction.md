# Experience direction

The desired Mouseion product character is a "digital scholarly reading desk."

This should not be interpreted as faux-historical styling or decorative
academic motifs.

Instead, Mouseion should feel:

- serious but approachable;
- scholarly without feeling institutional;
- centered on books, passages, vocabulary, and reading;
- information-dense when useful, but visually calm;
- typographically strong;
- restrained in color and ornament;
- respectful of bibliographic and linguistic information.

Avoid defaulting to generic SaaS aesthetics such as:

- interchangeable dashboard cards;
- excessive rounded containers;
- decorative gradients;
- oversized headings;
- unnecessary shadows;
- metrics without clear explanatory value.

Use visual hierarchy to communicate product priorities rather than decoration.

Sans-serif typography is appropriate for application controls and navigation.
Serif typography may be used where book text, quotations, or reading samples
benefit from stronger textual identity.
