# Reading-owned Book vocabulary and deck preparation

Status: **Accepted specification — implemented** · Date: 2026-10-06

Implementation status: issue #1505 binds new Book preparation, retries, and
re-preparation to the active Current-reading snapshot; issue #1506 freezes the
corpus-qualified two-occurrence exception; issue #1507 retires Browse selection
and Custom-deck access, and #1508 provides the backup-first data cutover for
unnamed Browse-selection rows.
Starting and finishing Reading do not depend on deck work. Cleanup of the
retained Custom-deck rows and artifacts, and measuring the provisional
cross-Book threshold, remain separate follow-ups.

This is the target selection, freeze, and retirement contract under [ADR 0085](../adr/0085-reading-owned-book-vocabulary.md). It amends the [Reading workflow](reading-workflow.md) and [frequency-floor decision](../adr/0048-frequency-floor-deck-selection.md); the implementation-status note above records what shipped and what remains separate. The historical [custom-deck feature contract](vocabulary-browse-concordance-and-custom-decks.md) is superseded for selection and custom decks. [Vocabulary Browse and Concordance](vocabulary-browse-and-concordance.md) retain their exploration purpose.

## Select once when Reading starts

- Starting or switching to a Book as Current reading freezes an immutable, owner- and language-scoped vocabulary snapshot from that Book's exact current completed analysis and source. A reread, or a return after End or Switch, makes a new snapshot that never adopts a released one; existing active and historical snapshots never change. Concurrent Known imports and occurrence decisions relative to a freeze resolve to a consistent durable outcome: a freeze sees either all or none of a committed change.
- Candidate identity is the effective `(study language, canonical lemma, analyzed POS)` after confirmed occurrence decisions. Count eligible occurrences in the Book's selected analysis scope. An identity qualifies if its in-Book count is **at least 3**, or if it is **exactly 2** in that Book and the sum across the learner's currently analyzed Books in that language is **at least 10**, including those two occurrences in this Book. Never combine POS or languages. Every Book can contribute, whether its disposition is Inbox or To Read, it is Current or Read, and it is Hidden or not, if it has current, complete, source-matching analysis. Do not use stale or partial evidence, another learner's Books, frozen snapshots, or external frequency datasets. A word seen once in the Book is excluded regardless of its cross-Book count.
- Use the same effective, eligible occurrence rules as Vocabulary Browse's per-Book counts, including confirmed corrections and exclusions, proper-name and POS exclusions, and analyzed scope. Imported lemma-wide Known vocabulary counts as Known for each POS. Known and active Reserved identities in this study language cannot be selected; Generated provenance alone is not an exclusion. Known/Reserved state never changes the occurrence counts. Preserve original analysis evidence and occurrence-decision provenance.
- The threshold **10 is provisional**; compare its marginal cards and Book-token coverage with a threshold of 7 on representative real per-Book counts before calling either validated. Report results separately by language and Book length rather than claiming a universal optimum. No production query was available during specification. Do not expose a learner-facing threshold control or imply a percentage coverage target. A future threshold change applies only to new freezes, not active or historical snapshots.
- If at least one twice-occurring candidate needs cross-Book counts and any contributing Book's projection is incomplete, starting or switching Reading cannot silently proceed with only the three-occurrence subset. Expose an explicit updating state, then an unavailable/retry state after bounded failure; keep the prior current reading unchanged on a failed switch. If there are no twice-occurring candidates, other Books' count readiness does not block selection. Never use incomplete totals as zero or stale rows as current. Counts are a read model; source evidence and exact occurrence decisions remain authoritative.

## Prepare from the reading, not the Book independently

- Starting Reading requires current analysis and, when relevant, ready cross-Book counts, **not** successful Anki/LLM preparation. Once the Book is Current reading, the learner can submit Book-deck preparation; every new preparation, retry, or re-preparation for that reading uses its frozen snapshot, rather than reselecting live vocabulary. Existing translation, sentence-quality, durable-job, and presentation-version rules still apply. A quality or meaning omission affects produced cards, not frozen selection or Reading's vocabulary transition; show omissions honestly.
- New Book-deck submissions are unavailable before the Book becomes Current reading and after it is ended, is switched away from, or is finished. Already-submitted jobs can complete; downloads of ready Book artifacts remain possible after Reading ends. Keep old preparations made through the former pre-reading path as historical, owner-scoped downloads; do not adopt their selected vocabulary into a later reading or offer new submissions through their old entry points. Pre-reading job routes must not remain an alternate way to create a deck.
- Finishing Reading accepts eligible frozen identities into modeled Known vocabulary with set semantics, whether preparation was never requested, failed, is pending, or omitted cards. Deck generation never marks Known. Later corpus growth, occurrence decisions, or Known imports cannot silently add to the snapshot. On reread, recalculate against then-current evidence and learner state, without overwriting earlier completions or artifacts.

## Retire independent custom decks

- Remove Browse Select/Remove, selected-count/review/clear/create controls and the independent custom-deck list, editing, preparation, status, and download UI. Retain Browse's current-Book counts, search, include-all control, truthful Known/Reserved/Book-deck state, and Concordance including sentence study. No custom/legacy deck listing, link, or download is shown, even if a stored deck exists. Old custom-deck page, mutation, status, and download HTTP routes cease to provide access; do not leave an unlinked creation or export endpoint active.
- Delete unnamed Browse-selection state as part of the cutover; do not convert it to Known or a Book snapshot. Refuse new custom submissions and edits. Allow jobs submitted before cutover to finish against their frozen inputs, but do not expose their artifacts in the retired UI/routes. Keep custom-deck rows, frozen specifications, outcomes, and APKG bytes temporarily, without access, until a separate inventory/backup and human-reviewed deletion plan is accepted. Already downloaded/imported APKGs cannot be revoked.
- Selection-row deletion is an owner-scoped, idempotent **data-only** cutover separate from structural DDL. Record its extent before deletion; retries must be safe, and recovery of deliberately discarded selections requires a pre-cutover backup, not recreation as Known vocabulary. Custom-deck records and APKG bytes are outside that deletion's scope.
- Rollout must distinguish target from current shipped functionality. Application route and view removal, existing-job handling, selection-data removal, and any later schema/data cleanup require separate validation; no change to the immutable baseline migration or shipped migrations. Do not assume the author's one test deck proves there are no other owners or data in production.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. A Book has an eligible word twice and other current analyzed Books in the same language have seven matching lemma/POS occurrences: total 9, excluded. One more matching occurrence makes 10, included in a **new** reading snapshot and every Book deck prepared from that snapshot. A singleton with 100 cross-Book occurrences stays excluded. Three in-Book occurrences need no cross-Book evidence to qualify.
2. Two matching occurrences in the current Book plus eight in a stale run, another language, another owner, or a different POS do not qualify. A correction changing an eligible occurrence's effective lemma moves only that occurrence between identities; an exclusion removes it. A Hidden Book's current matching analysis contributes too.
3. Missing count readiness with a twice-occurring candidate delays a reading start or switch with truthful recovery and no partial state. No twice-occurring candidates: the three-occurrence pool may be frozen even when another Book's counts are still rebuilding.
4. Preparing after Reading starts reuses the snapshot if other Books are later analyzed. Finishing with an omitted card, failed provider, or no deck still uses the frozen snapshot. End and reread: a new snapshot may differ; the first remains historical. Existing active readings remain unchanged after rollout.
5. Pre-reading Book-deck artifacts remain owner-downloadable but cannot become a new reading's snapshot or open a new pre-reading preparation. Submitted preparations can complete after Reading ends; new submissions cannot. No custom-deck UI or HTTP endpoint is accessible, even by old direct URL; in-flight custom jobs can complete without deleting stored results. Browse selection rows are removed without changing Known vocabulary.
