# Vocabulary Browse and Concordance

Status: **Accepted specification — implemented; Book-scoped Browse moved to the Book detail page at the Study cutover** · Updated: 2026-11-28

This is the current learner-facing contract for investigating vocabulary in a Book and its occurrences across the learner's Corpus. It replaces the [earlier combined specification](../archive/features/vocabulary-browse-concordance-and-custom-decks.md), whose saved-selection and custom-deck clauses are historical. Under [ADR 0136](../adr/0136-study-replaces-known-vocabulary-and-prepared-deck.md) the Current-reading snapshot and the deck rules of [Reading-owned Book vocabulary](../archive/features/reading-owned-book-vocabulary.md) are retired, and Book-scoped Browse is the word list of the **Book detail page** (`/books/{id}`), where it replaces the Reading working desk. Browse is read-only discovery: it edits nothing, and a fix starts only from a Wrong-word [Study](study.md) report.

## Evidence and identity

- The effective identity is `(study language, canonical lemma, analyzed POS)`, after owner-confirmed occurrence corrections and exclusions. Eligible occurrences have a valid lemma, `NOUN`, `VERB`, `ADJ`, or `ADV` POS, and pass the shared vocabulary token predicate; dependent separable particles do not become standalone vocabulary. Preserve immutable analyzer attribution and source text separately from the learner's effective decisions. Distinct senses of one lemma/POS are not separate identities.
- Use only an owned Book's source-matching **current completed analysis** and selected analyzed scope, including selected ancillary units. Any Book disposition can contribute to across-Book totals. Missing, stale, failed, and incomplete analyses contribute nothing; never substitute old runs or aggregate lemma records. Effective counts include singletons and exclude only ineligible or expressly excluded occurrences. Study state never alters counts.
- Browse is scoped to the one Book whose detail page is open, for any Book in My Books with a Book language. It has no independent Book picker or cross-Book fallback. An identity with no current eligible occurrence in that Book is absent even if evidenced elsewhere. Show explicit states for a Book with no completed analysis, an empty analyzed Book, an empty filtered page, counts updating, and counts unavailable after bounded retries. Each retains a useful route to the Book's analysis actions or retry, without implying that an unavailable count is zero.

## Browse

- Browse is the **Words** section of the Book detail page; it is not a page at `/vocabulary`, which opens Concordance. The retired Reading addresses redirect: `/reading?reading={bookID}` opens that Book's detail page, keeping the prefix, revision, row and page; a bare Reading address opens My Books. A malformed or out-of-range `page` returns 400 or 404 with an explanation and a First page link.
- Show the Book's title and language. Order by descending effective occurrences **in that Book**, then descending sum across all the owner's currently analyzed Books in the Book's language (including the Book), then canonical lemma and POS. Label both numbers **In this Book** and **Across analyzed books**, even when equal. This order is discovery, not a learning recommendation or token-coverage claim.
- Show each word's **Study state** in its own column: **Mature**, **In study** or **Not yet introduced**. Mature is the only state that counts as knowing a word. A word whose Study progress is withdrawn is not introduced until it is reintroduced. The list shows every word of the Book; Study state is a label, not a filter. Preserve only case-insensitive canonical-lemma prefix search, stable 25-result paging, and Book/POS-accurate links to Concordance. The list has no selection, review/clear, edit, or deck control, and no per-page Book/POS filters or alternate sort are required.
- Browse's owner- and Book-scoped effective count projection is governed by [ADR 0084](../adr/0084-browse-effective-count-projection.md). Counts for new analysis publish with the matching current-analysis pointer; corrections/exclusions update counts atomically when ready. For existing Books, gate results and across-Book totals until every contributing Book has a ready matching projection. Never return partial totals, stale counts, or a zero-result claim while rebuilding. A correction against an unready Book invalidates and retries its rebuild.

## Concordance and sentence study

- Browse opens Concordance in exact effective lemma/POS mode initially scoped to this Book; the learner can broaden to other Books of the Corpus or restrict to a selected subset. Direct lookup begins with exact observed surface and can switch explicitly to effective lemma/POS or **Analyzer lemma + POS (evidence)**. Effective lookup follows corrections and excludes omitted occurrences; observed-surface and analyzer-evidence modes show original evidence with correction/exclusion attribution. Never rewrite immutable source or analyzer identity as if a learner's correction changed it. For a language with a dedicated normalization profile, form search matches a stored, Go-computed profile key, so canonical-equivalence, case and final-sigma variants find the same results while accents and breathings stay significant and an unaccented query never matches an accented form.
- Allow optional grammar filters on the queried occurrence's own dependency relation or on the relation of its dependents. Results are a server-rendered KWIC list grouped by Book in title order. Each group has a left-hand Book title and occurrence count; dividers separate groups rather than individual occurrences. Within each row, left context is right-aligned against the observed target and right context begins immediately after it. Native `<details>`/`<summary>` disclosures reveal the complete flowing sentence, not duplicate technical metadata; a visibly labelled **Study** link opens a focused **Study this sentence** view. Missing syntax is explained without discarding a valid sentence. Return to the applied query, page, subset, and row when still present.
- The **Study this sentence** view is morphology-first. It shows the complete sentence, Book and structural location, the identified target, a **source-word-aligned morphology line**, a **notation key**, and a named, server-rendered **complete text alternative**. The line stacks each safely aligned word's curated core features (Case, Number, Gender, Person, Tense, Aspect, Mood, Voice, VerbForm, Degree) beneath it in one consistent order, using Leipzig abbreviations with analyzer-supplied alternatives joined by a slash; the words are static and are never a gloss, translation or whole-sentence claim.
- The line validates the sentence against its immutable source slice and aligns a word only when its span is valid, uniquely attributable, non-overlapping and exactly matching that slice. A shared-span, overlapping or mismatched word degrades locally while safe neighbours stay annotated; untrustworthy sentence bounds withhold the line and open the text alternative, without ever reconstructing the sentence.
- The **notation key** is a closed-by-default disclosure that lists only the labels this sentence uses, each with a plain-English explanation of the term. The **complete text alternative** is closed by default and opens by default only under whole-line fallback; it lists every analyzed word in source order with its surface, UPOS, analyzer and effective lemma, all stored morphology, dependency relation and head, and correction/exclusion state, keeping shared-span siblings and repeated forms distinguishable. The textual evidence is present in this named disclosure when evidence exists; a diagram may complement, never replace, it. An imported analysis shows a word's citation headword in place of the stored canonical key, and an analysis run that records no UD syntax reports **Syntax unavailable** in place of the dependency relation and head rather than a fabricated skeleton.
- A stored occurrence decision applies to a word only when it addresses exactly one persisted analyzed word, counted across its whole addressed scope before any filtering; otherwise it is shown separately and applied to none, never to every sibling and never chosen by lemma, POS or surface, and its proposed lemma is not presented as a confirmed effective outcome. A lemma correction never appears to correct morphology, and an exclusion never erases grammar evidence. The role-waypoint dependency chart and per-word source-token evidence (which would show contractions once with each word's features) remain future work.
- Keep one lookup strip with Find beside the term field and separate Book/grammar disclosures with explicit Apply actions. A one-sentence **Current results** summary states the *applied* mode, term/POS, Book subset, grammar filter, and range; editing draft controls never relabel old results. Preserve query state in stable 25-result server-rendered paging. Use native labeled forms, keyboard-reachable links, truthful no-JavaScript behavior, narrow-screen reflow, readable 400% zoom, and textual pending/failure recovery. HTMX may enhance but must not become the only path. An unsuccessful query must not replace earlier results with an unlabeled partial or false empty set.

### Shipped: captured Book priority and stable occurrence pages

Under [ADR 0086](../adr/0086-reading-working-desk-hidden-visibility-and-concordance.md), shipped Concordance searches every eligible current analysis in the active study language, and this block supersedes the Book-scope, grouping, and mode clauses above for that behavior.

- **Captured priority.** A fresh lookup captures the Book it came from, or the explicit absence `priority=none`, and carries it with the term, applied lemma/form meaning, optional exact POS, page, and evidence revision. An old Browse origin never supplies it. A request that carries `rev` without a priority, an unparsable priority, or a priority that is not an owned Book in the study language is malformed and gets the safe-restart notice.
- **Order and paging.** All priority-Book matches come first, then Books by case-insensitive title, exact title, and Book identity; within a Book by source unit, sentence, and token. One global sequence is paged at 25 with no sampling, per-Book caps, or interleaving: 60 priority hits occupy pages of 25, 25, then 10 before other Books. Each page-local group repeats its Book and an explicitly page-local count. The range line says *continues {Book} from the previous page* only when that group began earlier. No Book or corpus total is maintained.
- **Priority evidence.** *No matches in {Book}* is distinct from *has no current analysis to search*; neither produces an empty group and the other Books' results still follow.
- **Reading changes.** Read next, Finish and End do not reorder an existing result set. A Book detail page is addressed by its Book, so changing the Current reading does not change what a Browse origin names.
- **Evidence guard.** The revision covers current-analysis identity, corrections/exclusions, and titles. A mismatch returns *Current evidence changed* with Refresh; disposition, history and Study state do not change it. A valid page past the end says *No results on this page* and offers page 1.
- **Browse origin and return.** Browse rows hand Concordance a separate origin (`from_book`, `from_q`, `from_page`, `from_row`, `from_rev`, and `from_all` for links that predate the retired control): the Book, applied prefix, page, row identity, and the Browse evidence revision; study language is the ordinary `language` parameter. It survives exact lookup, new searches, refresh, paging, and Study, and never supplies captured priority. A direct visit has no origin. **Back to book vocabulary** opens the Book detail page, which restores the prefix, page and row focus while the evidence revision still matches. A changed revision requires **Refresh words** (keeps the prefix, page 1). A missing row alone explains and focuses the results heading. Study returns to the applied Concordance state with the same guards. Active-language mismatch takes precedence and lands in My Books.

## Acceptance and scale

- A lemma seen three times in a Book and once in another displays 3 and 4 on the first Book's detail page. Correcting those three away removes its row even if the other Book still contains it.
- A word the learner introduced and whose current review interval is at least 21 days shows **Mature**; a word introduced but not yet at that interval shows **In study**; a word with no Study progress shows **Not yet introduced**. The list never shows Known, Reserved or Unknown.
- Making a Book the Current reading, ending it or finishing it changes nothing in the Browse list or in Concordance's cross-Book evidence model. Browse offers investigation links but never saves an independent word selection or creates a deck. No custom/legacy deck link or direct URL remains as part of this feature.
- Enhanced requests expose pending text by about 1 second and end failed interactive work by 10 seconds with honest recovery.
