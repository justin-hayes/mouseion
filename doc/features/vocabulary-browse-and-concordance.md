# Vocabulary Browse and Concordance

Status: **Accepted specification — partially shipped** · Updated: 2026-10-06

This is the current learner-facing contract for investigating vocabulary in a Book and its occurrences across the learner's Books. It replaces the [earlier combined specification](vocabulary-browse-concordance-and-custom-decks.md), whose saved-selection and custom-deck clauses are historical under [ADR 0085](../adr/0085-reading-owned-book-vocabulary.md). The Book candidate/freeze rule is specified in [Reading-owned Book vocabulary](reading-owned-book-vocabulary.md). Browse is discovery, not a second deck-selection workflow.

## Evidence and identity

- The effective identity is `(study language, canonical lemma, analyzed POS)`, after owner-confirmed occurrence corrections and exclusions. Eligible occurrences have a valid lemma, `NOUN`, `VERB`, `ADJ`, or `ADV` POS, and pass the shared vocabulary token predicate; dependent separable particles do not become standalone vocabulary. Preserve immutable analyzer attribution and source text separately from the learner's effective decisions. Distinct senses of one lemma/POS are not separate identities.
- Use only an owned Book's source-matching **current completed analysis** and selected analyzed scope, including selected ancillary units. Any Book disposition can contribute to across-Book totals. Missing, stale, failed, and incomplete analyses contribute nothing; never substitute old runs, frozen Reading snapshots, or aggregate lemma records. Effective counts include singletons and exclude only ineligible or expressly excluded occurrences. Known, Reserved, and Generated vocabulary do not alter counts.
- Browse is scoped to the active study language's **Current reading Book**. It has no independent Book picker or cross-Book fallback. An identity with no current eligible occurrence in that Book is absent even if evidenced elsewhere. Show explicit states for no Current reading, unavailable/stale analysis, an empty analyzed Book, an empty filtered page, counts updating, and counts unavailable after bounded retries. Each retains a useful route to Reading or retry, without implying that an unavailable count is zero.

## Browse

- Show the Current reading's Book title and language. Order by descending effective occurrences **in that Book**, then descending sum across all the owner's currently analyzed Books in the active language (including the Book), then canonical lemma and POS. Label both numbers **In this Book** and **Across analyzed books**, even when equal. This order is discovery, not a learning recommendation or token-coverage claim. Show lemma/POS, counts, and truthful textual **Unknown**, **Known**, **Reserved**, or **Known · Reserved** state; imported lemma-wide Known identities are Known under every analyzed POS. An identity included in any successfully prepared Book deck for this Book has a separate **In a Book deck** note. Omitted cards and snapshot membership alone do not imply such inclusion.
- By default hide Known, Reserved, and identities included in a successfully prepared Book deck for this Book, including older preparations. One **Show already accounted-for words** control reveals all of them without changing counts or order. Preserve only case-insensitive canonical-lemma prefix search, stable 25-result paging, and Book/POS-accurate links to Concordance. Do not show Browse-selection count, Select/Remove, review/clear, or any path to custom or legacy decks. No per-page Book/POS filters or alternate sort are required.
- Browse's owner- and Book-scoped effective count projection is governed by [ADR 0084](../adr/0084-browse-effective-count-projection.md). Counts for new analysis publish with the matching current-analysis pointer; corrections/exclusions update counts atomically when ready. For existing Books, gate results and across-Book totals until every contributing Book has a ready matching projection. Never return partial totals, stale counts, or a zero-result claim while rebuilding. A correction against an unready Book invalidates and retries its rebuild. Book-candidate selection may proceed without cross-Book readiness only when the current Book has no twice-occurring candidate, as specified by the Reading-owned contract; this does not relax Browse's own readiness gate.

## Concordance and sentence study

- Browse opens Concordance in exact effective lemma/POS mode initially scoped to this Book; the learner can broaden to other current analyzed same-language Books or restrict to a selected subset. Direct lookup begins with exact observed surface and can switch explicitly to effective lemma/POS or **Analyzer lemma + POS (evidence)**. Effective lookup follows corrections and excludes omitted occurrences; observed-surface and analyzer-evidence modes show original evidence with correction/exclusion attribution. Never rewrite immutable source or analyzer identity as if a learner's correction changed it.
- Allow optional grammar filters on the queried occurrence's own dependency relation or on the relation of its dependents. Results are a server-rendered KWIC list grouped by Book in title order. Each group has a left-hand Book title and occurrence count; dividers separate groups rather than individual occurrences. Within each row, left context is right-aligned against the observed target and right context begins immediately after it. Native `<details>`/`<summary>` disclosures reveal the complete flowing sentence, not duplicate technical metadata; a visibly labelled **Study** link opens the exact sentence with Book/location, analyzer and effective token evidence, and textual dependency/head information. Missing syntax is explained without discarding a valid sentence. Return to the applied query, page, subset, and row when still present.
- Keep one lookup strip with Find beside the term field and separate Book/grammar disclosures with explicit Apply actions. A one-sentence **Current results** summary states the *applied* mode, term/POS, Book subset, grammar filter, and range; editing draft controls never relabel old results. Preserve query state in stable 25-result server-rendered paging. Use native labeled forms, keyboard-reachable links, truthful no-JavaScript behavior, narrow-screen reflow, readable 400% zoom, and textual pending/failure recovery. HTMX may enhance but must not become the only path. An unsuccessful query must not replace earlier results with an unlabeled partial or false empty set.

### Shipped: captured current-Book priority and stable occurrence pages

Under [ADR 0086](../adr/0086-reading-working-desk-hidden-visibility-and-concordance.md), shipped Concordance searches every eligible current analysis in the active study language, and this block supersedes the Book-scope, grouping, and mode clauses above for that behavior.

- **Captured priority.** A fresh lookup captures the then-current Book, or the explicit absence `priority=none`, and carries it with the term, applied lemma/form meaning, optional exact POS, page, and evidence revision. An old Browse origin never supplies it. A request that carries `rev` without a priority, an unparsable priority, or a priority that is not an owned Book in the study language is malformed and gets the safe-restart notice.
- **Order and paging.** All priority-Book matches come first, then Books by case-insensitive title, exact title, and Book identity; within a Book by source unit, sentence, and token. One global sequence is paged at 25 with no sampling, per-Book caps, or interleaving: 60 priority hits occupy pages of 25, 25, then 10 before other Books. Each page-local group repeats its Book and an explicitly page-local count. The range line says *continues {Book} from the previous page* only when that group began earlier. No Book or corpus total is maintained.
- **Priority evidence.** *No matches in {Book}* is distinct from *has no current analysis to search*; neither produces an empty group and the other Books' results still follow. A captured Book is described as the Book read at lookup time, never as current.
- **Reading changes.** Start, End, and Switch do not reorder an existing result set; the page explains that the reading changed and offers **Refresh results**, which resets to page 1 with current priority and evidence. An exact Browse identity (lemma with POS) keeps its meaning on refresh; an independent lookup recognizes its term again.
- **Evidence guard.** The revision covers current-analysis identity, corrections/exclusions, and titles. A mismatch returns *Current evidence changed* with Refresh; visibility, history, disposition, and accounting do not change it. A valid page past the end says *No results on this page* and offers page 1.

## Acceptance and scale

- A lemma seen three times in the Current reading Book and once in another displays 3 and 4. Correcting those three away removes its Browse row even if the other Book still contains it. Unknown includes previously generated words not Known or Reserved; the include-all control reveals accounted-for identities without altering their counts.
- Starting/stopping/switching Current reading changes Browse scope, not Concordance's cross-Book evidence model. Browse offers investigation links but never saves an independent word selection or creates a deck. No custom/legacy deck link or direct URL remains as part of this feature.
- On a documented warm local reference corpus of roughly 500 analyzed Books and 50 million tokens in one language, target p95 completed requests ≤2 seconds for Browse and ordinary Concordance lookup/filter/paging, and ≤5 seconds for grammar-filtered Concordance. Record hardware, source mix, workload, measurement boundary, and p95; never silently sample evidence to meet a deadline. Enhanced requests expose pending text by about 1 second and end failed interactive work by 10 seconds with honest recovery. These are acceptance targets, not claims of shipped measurements.
