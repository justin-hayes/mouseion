# Archived Ancient Greek design (2026-10)

The earlier Ancient Greek design effort is withdrawn and kept here for
provenance only. It was never implemented, and none of it is part of Mouseion's
current product contract or binding on future work.

It is superseded by the Ancient Greek MVP spec
[#2098](https://github.com/justin-hayes/mouseion/issues/2098), with the decision
history in the restart brief
[#2097](https://github.com/justin-hayes/mouseion/issues/2097). The current
decisions live in [`doc/product.md`](../../product.md), the live ADRs under
[`doc/adr/`](../../adr/), and the domain glossary in
[`CONTEXT.md`](../../../CONTEXT.md).
