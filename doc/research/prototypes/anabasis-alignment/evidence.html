Limits and accepted prototype conclusion


Purposive, proposal-biased sample; no independent annotation or untouched test set. The statistical comparison is a small from-scratch IBM Model 1 baseline, not fast_align/eflomal itself. The dictionary method matches words from all compatible glosses, not contextual senses. Existing Ugarit links are partial, contributor records with provenance qualifications.


Justin accepted these baselines as review suggestions only, not an unattended replacement. For an uncertain word link, leave confirmed English unemphasised. For an uncertain sentence span, use labelled wider-passage context without emphasis, or withhold English if even that context is unreliable. The overall Greek provider decision remains separate; better evidence as LLM context is also a possible benefit, not a measured result or an authorised payload change.
