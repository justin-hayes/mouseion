# ADR 0085: Reading owns Book vocabulary and deck preparation

Status: **Accepted (implemented)** · Date: 2026-10-06 · Implemented: #1509, #1515, #1516 · Author: Justin + OpenCode

Amended by [ADR 0086](0086-reading-working-desk-hidden-visibility-and-concordance.md) §9: Browse lives in Reading and Concordance in Vocabulary, and "any Book disposition" counts Inbox and To Read, Hidden or not.

Supersedes the active product boundary of [ADR 0082](0082-independent-custom-vocabulary-decks.md), amends the Book-selection rule in [ADR 0048](0048-frequency-floor-deck-selection.md), and narrows deck submission under [ADR 0078](0078-book-dispositions-and-current-reading.md). Existing artifacts and snapshots retain their provenance. The [feature contract](../features/reading-owned-book-vocabulary.md) owns the cutover and acceptance details.

## Context

Independent cross-Book shortlists and custom-deck preparations add a second selection, generation, and user-interface lifecycle that distracts from reading and finishing Books. The established Book-local floor of three avoids the rare-word tail, but misses words seen twice in a Book that recur across the learner's analyzed Books. Selecting independently when a Book deck is prepared and when Reading starts can also produce different vocabulary sets as evidence changes. An external frequency dataset was deliberately removed by [ADR 0018](0018-remove-dwds-frequency.md); the relevant corpus is the learner's own current same-language analysis evidence.

## Decision

Starting or switching to a **Current reading** freezes one immutable Book vocabulary snapshot. An eligible effective `(study language, canonical lemma, analyzed POS)` identity enters if it occurs at least three times in that Book, or exactly twice there and at least ten times in total across the owner's currently analyzed same-language Books, **including the current Book**. Count effective eligible occurrences after confirmed corrections/exclusions; count any Book disposition, but no stale/incomplete analysis. Exclude current Known and Reserved vocabulary as before, not Generated provenance. Ten is a provisional default to compare with seven on real per-Book data; neither is claimed to optimize deck size without measurement. This selection makes no token-coverage guarantee and does not change analysis-time token eligibility or coverage denominators.

Only a Current reading can initiate a new Book-deck preparation. Its preparation uses that reading's frozen snapshot, not a second live selection; no deck is required to start, finish, or accept vocabulary from Reading. Preparation and its card omissions do not alter the snapshot. If cross-Book counts are not ready and at least one twice-occurring identity needs them, starting/switching waits with an explicit updating/unavailable recovery state, rather than freezing an incomplete set. When there are no twice-occurring candidates, other Books' count readiness does not block the freeze. Later analyses, corrections, or changes to Known vocabulary do not rewrite active or historical snapshots; a reread freezes anew. New submissions stop when that Book leaves Current reading; already-submitted work may finish, and existing Book artifacts remain downloadable, including those made by the old pre-reading path. Remove the pre-reading submission entry points, without deleting historical Book artifacts.

Retire the learner-facing custom-deck and unnamed Browse-selection workflows. Browse and Concordance remain for exploring vocabulary and evidence without selection controls. Delete unnamed Browse-selection state; stop new custom-deck creation, edits, preparation, and owner-facing status/download routes. Jobs submitted before the cutover may finish. Retain custom-deck records and artifacts temporarily **without UI or HTTP access**, pending a separately reviewed, audited, backed-up cleanup; this decision does not authorize deleting them or affect APKGs already downloaded or imported into Anki. Historical custom-deck artifacts never become Book snapshots or Known vocabulary.

## Why this trade-off

One Reading-owned freeze makes the modeled knowledge transition and its optional study artifact refer to the same selected identities without making reading depend on LLM translation or Anki. A corpus-qualified two-occurrence exception increases the chance that additional cards help with later Books without restoring the one- and two-occurrence long tail wholesale. It adds a dependency on complete cross-Book counts for some reading starts, and an evolving personal corpus means two different readings of the same Book can select differently. Retiring cross-Book custom decks sacrifices arbitrary learner shortlists to keep the product centered on finishing Books; retaining inaccessible data during cutover avoids unverified irreversible deletion.

## Consequences

- The original three-occurrence route remains sufficient; a singleton is never included by a high cross-Book total. Cross-Book counts are per effective lemma **and** POS, owner, and study language.
- Frozen Reading vocabulary is distinct from the cards actually produced: omissions can leave selected identities without Anki cards; finishing still accepts eligible snapshot identities into modeled Known vocabulary. Generating cards never marks them Known.
- Existing active readings do not gain two-occurrence identities. Already-imported Anki packages and historical Book-deck downloads remain unaffected. The old custom-deck UI/routes cease, even though their stored rows persist temporarily.
- ADR 0082 and the selection/custom-deck portions of the earlier [Vocabulary specification](../features/vocabulary-browse-concordance-and-custom-decks.md) are historical, not competing instructions. The current Browse/Concordance contract is [here](../features/vocabulary-browse-and-concordance.md). Schema cleanup, if later approved, follows ADR 0038/0070 with a separate migration and human review; neither the immutable baseline nor shipped migrations are rewritten.
