# Study material to check: 20 words from *Der ferne Spiegel*

For each word: is the **Learned meaning** right for sentence 1, does every sentence use the word in that meaning,
and is each translation faithful? Tick the box or write a correction under the word. `[[ ]]` marks the tested word.
